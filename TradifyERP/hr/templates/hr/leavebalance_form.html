About Leave Balances
Fields
  • Employee: Select the employee
  • Leave Type: Select the leave type
  • Year: Leave balance year
  • Opening Balance: Carryover from previous year
  • Allocated: Annual allocation for this year
  • Taken: Days already used
Available Balance

The system automatically calculates available balance as:
Opening + Allocated - Taken

Example
  • Opening: 5 days (carryover)
  • Allocated: 20 days (annual)
  • Taken: 10 days (used)
  • Available: 15 days
Important
  • Only one leave balance per employee per leave type per year
  • Leave requests require a valid balance
  • Update this when employees use leave
  • Contact HR before manually editing
