ℹ️ About Cash Flow Statements

A cash flow statement shows how cash moves in and out of your business over a specific period.

Three Main Categories:
  • Operating Activities: Day-to-day business activities (sales, expenses)
  • Investing Activities: Asset purchases/sales, investments
  • Financing Activities: Loans, capital contributions, dividends

The system automatically generates statements from your General Ledger data.
