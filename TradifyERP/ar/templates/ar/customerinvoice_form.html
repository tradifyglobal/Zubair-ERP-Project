Create New Invoice

Create a new customer invoice to track sales and receivables.

Required Fields:
  • Customer: Select the customer
  • Invoice Number: Unique invoice identifier
  • Invoice Date: Date of invoice issuance
  • Due Date: Payment due date
  • Total Amount: Invoice total amount
Optional Fields:
  • Reference Number: External reference or PO number
  • Currency: Invoice currency (if multi-currency)
  • Subject: Invoice title or subject line
  • Notes: Additional terms, comments, or instructions
Note: All invoices are automatically logged in the audit trail.
