Double-Entry Booking

In double-entry bookkeeping, every transaction must have equal debit and credit amounts.

Rule: Debits must equal Credits
Steps:
  1. Enter the transaction date
  2. Describe the transaction
  3. Select debit account and amount
  4. Select credit account and amount
  5. Ensure debit = credit
  6. Click Save Entry
Tip: The system will validate that debits equal credits before saving.
