Ratio Interpretation Guide
Liquidity Ratios

Current Ratio: Measures ability to pay short-term debts. Ideal range: 1.5-3.0

Quick Ratio: More conservative measure of immediate liquidity. Ideal: 0.5-1.0

Solvency & Leverage Ratios

Debt-to-Equity: Shows financial leverage. Lower values indicate less risk.

Debt Ratio: Percentage of assets financed by debt. Lower is generally better.
