Single Entry Mode

Create individual ledger entries one at a time. Use this for simple transactions.

Note: For balanced transactions, use Quick Add Entry to ensure debit = credit.
Steps:
  1. Select the transaction date
  2. Choose the account to post to
  3. Select DEBIT or CREDIT
  4. Enter the amount
  5. Describe the transaction
  6. Click Save Entry
